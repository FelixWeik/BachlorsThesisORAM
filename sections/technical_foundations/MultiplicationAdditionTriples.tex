\label{sub:MultiplicationAdditionTriples}
AND Triples on vectors and Dot-Product Triples on additive shares, help facilitate computing (bitwise) AND and dot products on secret shares \cite{vadapalli2023duoram}.
Due to their construction they are often called \textit{Beaver like triples}.

This research will use both the definitions of AND Triples and Dot-Product Triples by Vadapalli et al. \cite{vadapalli2023duoram}.
\begin{quote}
    \textit{AND-Triples}:
    Let $(X_0,Y_0,Z_0)\in \{0,1\}^\lambda\times\{0,1\}^\lambda\times\{0,1\}^\lambda$ and $(X_1,Y_1,Z_1)\in \{0,1\}^\lambda\times\{0,1\}^\lambda\times\{0,1\}^\lambda$.
    The triples are called \textit{AND-Triples} if for a random $T\in\{0,1\}^\lambda$ the following holds true:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item $Z_0=(X_0\land Y_0)\oplus T$ and
        \item $Z_1 = (X_1\land Y_0)\oplus T$
    \end{enumerate}
\end{quote}

\begin{quote}
    \textit{Dot-Product Triples}:
    Two triples $(X_0,Y_0,Z_0)\in Z^n_{2^w}\times Z^n_{2^w}\times Z^n_{2^w}$ and $(X_1,Y_1,Z_1)\in Z^n_{2^w}\times Z^n_{2^w}\times Z^n_{2^w}$ are called \textit{Dot-Product Triples} if for a random $T\in Z^n_{2^w}$ the following holds true:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item $Z_0=\langle X_0,Y_1\rangle+T$ and
        \item $Z_1=\langle X_1,Y_0\rangle+T$
    \end{enumerate}
\end{quote}

Just like with Beaver Triples, performing an AND or dot-product operation will consume one triple.
Therefore, again, one can precompute the communication expensive parts and perform the safe MPC operation on the real values lateron.

The implementation (\ref{ch:Implementation}) uses the \textit{Du-Atallah Protocol} to calculate its AND and dot-product triples.
It is a \textit{(2+1)-MPC Protocol}, often called \textit{Server-Adided 2-MPC}, which describes a 2-party MPC protocol with a third party \cite{vadapalli2023duoram}.
This third party does not collude with the other two parties and facilitates the MPC by sending some correlated randomness to the two MPC parties.
A more explicit description of the Du-Atallah Protocol can be found in the according paper by Du and Atallah \cite{ata01priv}.
